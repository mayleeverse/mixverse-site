Syaski dances to the song while the notes fly in. Hit each one as it lands, right on the beat.

A and S are your left hand, L and ; your right. SPACE is the heart: hit it when the two halves meet over her head. Hold the key down for long notes and let go at the end.

The notes on the right are flying in to the beat of BAKA!! 2.0. Click here, then hit A, S, L, ; or SPACE as they reach the rings. Or tap a key.
