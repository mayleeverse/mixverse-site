HIT THEBEAT!

Syaski dances to the song while the notes fly in. Hit each one as it lands, right on the beat.

Your left hand takes A and S, your right takes L and ;. SPACE is for the heart: hit it when the two halves meet over her head. Hold the key down for long notes and let go at the end.

    Download for Windows .ZIP · ABOUT 150 MB

    MiXVERSE runs on your PC. It isn't playable in a web browser.

    INSTALL

    1. Download the .zip.
    2. Right-click it and choose Extract All.
    3. Open the folder and double-click MiXVERSE.exe.
    4. If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway.
